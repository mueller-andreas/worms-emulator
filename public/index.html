    How to play

    • Move: ← → or A / D
    • Jump: Enter
    • Aim: ↑ ↓ (or move the mouse)
    • Fire: hold Space (or mouse button) to charge power, release to shoot
    • Weapons: 1 Bazooka · 2 Grenade · 3 Shotgun · 4 Air Strike
    • Watch the wind — it pushes your bazooka rockets!
    • Last team with a worm standing wins.
